Subject: Proposal — Reseller Performance Optimization Engine to Unlock ₹2.10 Cr/month Net GMV

### Objective
Activate high-potential dormant resellers and monetize under-leveraged social influencers to drive **profitable, FCF-positive GMV growth** — aligned with Meesho’s 2024–25 strategic pillar: “Deepening Reseller Engagement.”

### Key Insight
Analysis of 5,000 resellers reveals:
- **14.3% are “Ghost Resellers”**: High historical GMV (≥₹5,000) but inactive (>30 days)  
- **15.1% are “Social Catalysts”**: High sharing (≥50 shares) but low GMV → under-monetized  
- **17.1% are “Niche Specialists”**: Focused in Home/Beauty — fastest-growing categories  

These segments represent a **₹2.12 Cr/month GMV opportunity**.

### Proposed Interventions
| Segment | Intervention | Cost | Expected GMV Uplift |
|--------|-------------|------|---------------------|
| Ghost Reseller | ₹20 cashback + WhatsApp nudge (“Your top buyer just purchased!”) | ₹0.021 Cr | ₹0.86 Cr |
| Social Catalyst | “Top Reseller” badge + early access to trending catalog | ₹0 | ₹0.75 Cr |
| Niche Specialist | Category-specific training (“How to sell more Home products”) | ₹0 | ₹0.51 Cr |
| **Total** | | **₹0.021 Cr** | **₹2.12 Cr** |

**Net Impact**: **₹2.10 Cr/month** at near-zero marginal cost.

### Validation Plan
- **A/B Test**: 2,400 Ghost Resellers (treatment) vs. 2,400 (control)  
- **Primary Metric**: Reseller-Led GMV per reseller (30-day lift ≥8%)  
- **Guardrails**: No increase in returns (>2%) or support tickets (>5%)  
- **ROI Positive**: (GMV uplift × 10% margin) > incentive cost

### Why This Matters
- Fully leverages **existing infrastructure** (cashback wallet, WhatsApp, badges)  
- Directly supports **Meesho’s FCF-first philosophy** (Letter from CEO, p. 3)  
- Scalable to full reseller base → **₹2.10 Cr/month net GMV**
